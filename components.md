# Components and Their Functions

| Component | Purpose |
|---|---|
| NPN Transistors | Act as electronic switches |
| DC Motor | Converts electrical energy into mechanical motion |
| Push Buttons | Control the switching operation |
| 9V Battery | Provides electrical power |
| Connecting Wires | Establish electrical connections |

## Simulation
The circuit was designed and tested using Tinkercad Circuits.

## Project Functionality
- Forward rotation
- Reverse rotation
- Motor control through switching combinations
